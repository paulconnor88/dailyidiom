Simplyhealth
Product architecture prototype — internal

1 product. Different levels of care.

Members see one experience, through one brand, in one app. Employers choose the level of healthcare access they want to provide.
The shift: We stop selling named constructs (Essentials, On-Demand, Optimise) — which research is showing creates both internal and external confusion — and move to selling Simplyhealth as one product, one experience, with different access levels to healthcare.
All levels
Each level builds on the one before. Every employee sees one app, one brand — features unlock based on what you choose.
